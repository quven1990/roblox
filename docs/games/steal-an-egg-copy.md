### Right game

Steal An Egg is a 7-player pet / tycoon loop: you steal eggs, hatch them into pets, and the pets make money. Train on the treadmill to raise Speed, upgrade the base, and steal from other players. Official description also lists rarer eggs, pet sizes, and mutations.

If you landed here looking for Steal a Brainrot codes, that is a different game.

### How a run works

1. Steal an egg from a pet, a nest, or another player.
2. Get it back to your base. The steal only counts if you keep it.
3. Hatch the egg. Pets pay you over time.
4. Spend coins on the treadmill and base so you can reach better eggs.
5. Repeat. Steal mutated or high-size pets when you see them — they travel with the pet.

### FAQ

**Is this Steal a Brainrot?** No. Different developer, different units. Brainrot has a redeem panel. This game currently does not.

**Are there Steal An Egg codes?** Not as of 2026-08-15.

**How do you make money?** Pets generate coins. Hatch, keep high earners, sell or lose the rest when you steal.

**What does the treadmill do?** It raises Speed. Faster movement is how you reach more eggs and get out of a steal.

**Where is the pet list?** `/steal-an-egg/pets` goes live after the Index is logged. Linking a fake King Mammoth tier list is worse than a short wait.
