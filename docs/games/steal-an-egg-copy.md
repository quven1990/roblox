### Right game

Steal An Egg is a 7-player pet / tycoon loop: you steal eggs, hatch them into pets, and the pets make money. Train on the treadmill to raise Speed, upgrade the base, and steal from other players. Official description also lists rarer eggs, pet sizes, and mutations.

If you landed here looking for Steal a Brainrot codes, that is a different game. This shop can still sell brainrot-named eggs — those pets belong here, not to Brainrot.

### How a run works

1. Steal an egg from a biome pet, a nest, or another player.
2. Get it back to your base. The steal only counts if you keep it.
3. Hatch on a timer (Robux can skip growth). Pets pay you in $/s.
4. Spend coins on the treadmill, trails, and pen so you can reach later biomes.
5. When egg reset hits, contest the scramble. Late game, skip tiny eggs and take ones about 2–3× your character.

### Map and Speed gates

Zones sit behind Speed. Forest and Lake are on the in-game Index. Later names come from gameplay, not from Index labels. Do not publish a Recommended Speed number ladder.

1. Forest — Index biome. Eight pet slots. Tutorial chicken.
2. Lake — Index biome. Frog is the first visible slot.
3. Desert — Gameplay. Early Speed gate.
4. Jungle — Gameplay. Tiger chase.
5. Yeti / ice pocket — Gameplay. Snow area; Yeti chase.
6. Volcano — Gameplay. Scorpion-scale chase in one recording.
7. Abyss Ocean — Gameplay. Whale-scale eggs.
8. Prehistoric — Gameplay. T-Rex / dino chase.
9. Cosmic King / final cosmic — Last Speed wall. One recording showed ~700 million Recommended Speed.

### Eggs, shop, and resets

Wild eggs come from biome pets. Steal prompts can name the take (King Mammoth, Huge Gorilla). Shop sells separate eggs, including brainrot-named stock. Shop names are not Index rows until they appear in Pet Index.

UI can say egg reset (~5 minutes in late-game footage). A moon event also fires. Huge / giant are egg size. No 3-hour guaranteed Huge clock. Grow All / Instant Grow can skip hatch; one huge egg sat ~11 minutes.
